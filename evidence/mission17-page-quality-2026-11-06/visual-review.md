### Moor & Meadow

The genuine Mission16 desktop header had ten flat links over two rows. The current primary navigation and grouped service hierarchy are visibly calmer and preserve access to each page. Compact mobile navigation already existed in Mission16; Mission17 improves its hierarchy and current-page context rather than inventing that capability.

Home now provides exact first-paragraph service overviews with real detail-page links, leaving complete wording and selected photographs on the original service records. The maintenance page has a useful image/copy relationship, readable mobile sequence and specific garden-conversation action. About leads with the supplied philosophy about gardens working for their users instead of an empty About opening followed by another About heading. Contact now presents the genuine contact section directly, without duplicate opening or a generic exploration block.

This is credible, not exceptional. The Home still follows a familiar opening/service-list/About/contact rhythm. Two approved service paragraphs and licensed public-garden illustrations do not establish detailed service scope, completed projects or an owner biography. Layout cannot repair that evidence gap by manufacturing claims.

### Ember Festival Kitchen

The true before/after shows the menu moving from after services and About to immediately after the opening. Visitors reach exact supplied items, descriptions and prices sooner. Short linked service overviews reduce repetition. About foregrounds the food philosophy and offers a useful Menu destination. Final Menu navigation, breadcrumb and visible heading now agree, with serif typography related to the Home. The final Contact page also removes the earlier mismatched generic opening and unnecessary exploration block.

The remaining dominant plate photograph has informal lighting and busy surroundings. Removing a competing Home service image makes the page calmer, but cannot turn unrelated licensed illustrations into a coherent commissioned shoot or show they depict this business’s actual food. The two approved menu items and short story provide limited commercial substance. The service-box and About structures remain recognisable. Automatic SEO title source remains unchanged rather than silently rewriting owner metadata to match the reviewed visible Menu presentation; further metadata changes need original review.

### Quiet Light Studio

The sidebar, expressive serif type, restrained cool palette and asymmetrical service hierarchy make the no-photo choice feel intentional. About gives “Clarity comes before decoration” proper prominence. The mobile service page presents complete exact wording, clearly shared business-method context and a supported project-conversation action.

The earlier desktop service body was squeezed into a narrow strip with a large empty centre. Final rendered evidence confirms a usable main reading column and distinct secondary method rail. The browser regression measures approximately 516px of body width and a 70px rail gap, independently of the heading. This is a real correction, not just a green source assertion.

The result remains restrained rather than exceptional. Much of its voice comes from carefully supplied fixture wording. Reusing the business method across service pages adds continuity, not new service-specific detail, deliverables or proof of results.

## Sparse-source decisions

All three sparse sites are **Needs further design improvement** as complete multi-page results. Their Home pages are clean, readable, honest image-free previews. None fabricates missing services, photography or biography. However, they visibly share Home/Services/Contact navigation, a brand opening, “What we do” with one service name, and “Get in touch”. Their separate Services pages repeat that name beneath Services, “What we do” and a generic exploration conclusion, without descriptive service facts. The extra route adds little visitor value.

Mission17 deliberately preserves those existing routes for the controlled same-route comparison; it has not solved page necessity for name-only sources. The next improvement should let the original planner retain a strong concise Home until another page has a real purpose, or ask one optional useful question about the service. Invented copy would conceal the problem rather than solve it.

## Whole-site acceptance and next priority

The rich businesses have meaningful identity and Home-composition differences. Their About pages nevertheless share a large owner statement, offset supporting paragraph and two-destination conclusion. Contact is now clearer but remains a deliberately simple shared contact composition. The service-detail family is recognisable despite image-led versus image-free treatments. This is substantial improvement in original-word presentation and customer journeys, not proof that every page of every ten-page site feels individually commissioned.

The highest-value next work is source-aware page necessity and useful service depth, followed by a bounded creative comparison that evaluates whole-page coherence without inventing facts. The current governed proposal boundary remains Home-focused; whole-site architecture or original service-copy generation requires separate schema and authority review. A valid model schema would not establish good design, and no live model was used here.

Technical, factual, owner and release acceptance remain separate. Neither this review nor automated accessibility/SEO/navigation checks establish complete accessibility certification, public release readiness or Google ranking. Free and paid previews must retain the same original design quality.
