## Moor & Meadow

The soft green, cream and deep green palette, serif headings and quiet photographic opening form a coherent garden identity. The Home page balances the opening photograph with an understandable service hierarchy, story and relevant conversation CTA. The About page gives its approved philosophy more prominence and a different photo/text arrangement. The compact phone Menu now leaves the actual opening visible instead of consuming the first screen. Text remains easy to follow on the inspected phone pages.

The maintenance page remains a relatively ordinary two-part page: two service paragraphs followed by a general garden philosophy and enquiry block. Its lower section repeats ideas already visible elsewhere instead of turning approved practical details into a more useful service-specific reading journey. This is a planning/art-direction limitation rather than an absence of source facts: access, seasonal priorities and scope are already supplied. The Japanese/public-garden and conservatory illustrations are attractive but cannot establish Devon work or a portfolio. That authenticity gap is a source limitation and is honestly disclosed.

## Ember Festival Kitchen

The brown, cream and warm gradient palette is coherent across Home, Menu, About and Contact. The menu is now easy to find and its two categories, exact prices and practical enquiry route are legible. The About page and Contact request ask about the gathering and useful details rather than suggesting unsupported checkout or booking. Phone navigation is substantially cleaner.

The dominant casual plated-food photographs are the largest remaining weakness. The meat-and-potatoes hero and curry-like buffet image do not convincingly tell the supplied vegetable-bowl/festival-menu story. Their captions make their illustrative status truthful, but captions do not make the photographic narrative commercially appropriate. Missing authentic menu/catering photos are a source limitation; choosing to make these mismatched illustrations the dominant visual story is also an art-direction weakness. A deliberately image-free, food-typography direction could be a better supported alternative until suitable imagery exists.

The two-item approved menu necessarily limits richness. There is still conspicuous space around a small amount of menu information, and the story language becomes cautious or general in places. The existing source includes useful gathering and practical enquiry information, so presentation and wording can improve without fabricating claims. Preserve deliberate owner wording and prepare any rewrite for review. This remains a credible prototype but does not yet feel like a memorable commissioned food website.

## Quiet Light Studio

The corrected system-sans headings with serif body text, deep blue-green opening, cream sections and desktop navigation rail give the studio a recognisable image-free identity. The oversized statements are deliberate rather than empty photograph placeholders. The About page's supplied process is presented as an editorial hierarchy and numbered sequence; it feels meaningfully different from Moor's photographic About page. Relevant email/project conversation destinations are clear.

Some phone typography is too emphatic for the available measure. The Home headline starts with a lone “Make” line, and “Clarity comes before decoration” occupies four large lines. These are not clipping defects, but the wrapping and scale lack the finesse expected from exceptional mobile art direction. The Brand direction service page still repeats the same two-section explanation/CTA skeleton as other service-detail pages. Its supplied two paragraphs are preserved and useful, but the visitor journey remains brief and predictable. The representation now permits more nuanced choices; the current fixture does not fully demonstrate them.

## Yard & Thread

Chartreuse, navy, large sans type and an inset opening statement create a markedly different same-industry website from Moor. The absence of photographs feels intentional. The small-space/container offer is clear, and the service page's alternating sections make the three services easy to distinguish. Its enquiry language is practical and appropriate to the source.

The very bright opening is a strong taste-dependent brand decision, not a universal definition of premium. The service page still follows three similarly weighted text bands, and the broad concluding question is another large text/CTA block. It is individual enough for a professional private preview, but not yet a particularly rich or surprising commissioned design. About and Contact were not present in the available compressed after subset, so no full-site aesthetic acceptance is claimed for those routes.

## Shared findings and next comparison

The four sites show genuine whole-page differences: photo-led calm gardening, warm menu-led catering, restrained image-free studio with a navigation rail, and bold image-free small-space gardening. Moor versus Yard is meaningful same-industry variation, beyond names, colour swaps or replacement photographs.

Recognisable patterns remain below the opening: service explanation followed by a general CTA block, repeated section-sized statements and fairly similar email conclusions. Short desktop Contact pages leave the footer above a pale unused area at the bottom of the viewport. This is a visible polish limitation, even though the contact routes and factual content are sound. Richer source facts help, but current composition decisions also leave room for improvement.

The next valuable experiment is a source-controlled model proposal using this expressive grammar, judged from complete phone/desktop output against the real deterministic result. It should test whether the model can improve service-page information architecture, mobile type scale/wrapping and distinctive section pacing without inventing facts. For photography-led businesses it should also be able to omit a weak illustration rather than use an available image merely because it exists. Preserve inferior or invalid output honestly. Schema validity, technical success and these hand-authored fixtures cannot establish model superiority or exceptional design consistency.
