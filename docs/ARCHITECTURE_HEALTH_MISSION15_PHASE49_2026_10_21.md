## Approval, scope and correction boundaries

Quote preparation uses the original listing-reference parser, publicBusinessContent and prepareBusinessContentEnquiry validators over the authenticated content_list result. The reference is customer-supplied selection, never authority. Stable ID, published revision and optional variant must resolve exactly. Archived/cancelled/expired, removed, foreign, duplicate or changed records cannot be accepted. Exact customer, business, role, source object/stamp, current revision and typing epoch are rechecked around asynchronous reads and owner acceptance. Greeting context also comes from the exact checked listing rather than a different historic customer service. Existing amount/date remain unchanged; listed prices remain informational and requests remain unconfirmed.

Newsletter source review reuses original Brand DNA and source preparation, not another copy engine. The same service ID or selected item/option is retained; a newer published revision is displayed for deliberate review and re-read before acceptance. Missing services/options do not fall back to similarly named records. Only source evidence changes; exact edited subject/body/photos/references/channels/brief and selected target remain. Existing saved record is unchanged until a separate original owner save. Manual review metadata explicitly says wording is not automatically verified. Account confirmation of an old saved record does not confirm current edits or new source evidence.

Current approved corrections can therefore improve future quote/reply/content preparation through existing shared profile/catalogue records without creating durable memory or implying model retraining. Work and Creator retain their specialist approval/mutation authority.
