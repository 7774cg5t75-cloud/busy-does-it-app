# Authentic business storytelling and full-page art direction

The Website Builder now prepares an explicit, reviewed whole-page direction using original owner words and exact approved sources. It also accepts a bounded optional business story, including when the starting website has no About section. These changes are private source-level capabilities. Nothing was published or activated.

## What changed

- Added authored whole-page presentation through the existing finite hero-variation contract. The eight original hero families remain unchanged and expandable. There is no new renderer, profile store, asset library, provider or execution authority.
- Coordinated service arrangements include a featured menu-like introduction, a practical service board, spacious studio rows, offset entries and a photographic mosaic treatment. Selection follows the existing reviewed family; explicit service and story choices stay in place until the owner deliberately compares coordinated alternatives.
- Added spread, working-method and typographic manifesto story treatments; stronger contact/footer continuity; responsive gallery/feature geometry; and category-led approved product/menu snapshots with exact original prices. Live product-reader permissions and checkout restrictions are unchanged. The new menu treatment is a private snapshot presentation, not a new live ordering contract.
- The guided editor supports optional owner-written story, source-bound introduction/action/heading suggestions, and deliberate grouping of exactly three distinct eligible opening photographs. Ambiguous, foreign, stale, unsuitable or unavailable selections fail closed. Other gallery images are never silently replaced.
- New owner requests include individual/elegant whole-page proposals, preserving a headline while comparing its surroundings, an alternative direction and a more prominent existing menu. Recognition remains finite; no model calls occurred.
