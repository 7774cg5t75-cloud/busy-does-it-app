## Five substantial connected improvements

1. **Private reply device save and deliberate reopen.** An explicit owner action stores one versioned private reply field on the original customer record through the original scoped business snapshot/cache envelope and checkpoint queue. It includes owner/business/customer, original filing reference, source snapshot revision, exact source comparison, wording and purpose. Encoding failure is distinct from unknown write/readback. Confirmed byte readback establishes a device copy; the UI does not claim account persistence. Unknown outcomes permit read-only reconciliation, not blind write replay. Inbox reopening uses the exact filed source and existing Keep/Discard protection. New typing survives an older save, and only the saved words are acknowledged. Duplicate clicks, current role/scope, source, customer, payload and revision checks protect the original parent path.
2. **Owner announcement and offer poster/advert wording.** Original finite Content Creator preparation now accepts owner-supplied facts for social, poster and advert text. Actual dates, prices, restrictions and conditions are retained verbatim within the selected treatment; missing facts are requested rather than invented. Brand DNA and current approved identity can supply presentation. Owner claims remain explicitly not independently verified. Source/format/treatment flow through original typed/transcribed routing, pending review, Keep/Use, editing and private save/reopen. Format changes preserve owner facts and existing photos/channels until deliberate acceptance. Text is not a rendered poster, video, live advert or campaign. Existing Growth service/business/catalogue format paths remain compatible; no new owner-announcement Growth recommendation was fabricated.
3. **More relevant private replies.** Original enquiry-question classification produces useful review checks for price, availability, location/accessibility and options. Acknowledgement wording reflects those finite categories without executing or copying embedded source instructions. Owners can deliberately explain the exact current approved service description only when a unique matching approved service exists. Duplicate, missing, foreign or different service facts cannot supply that explanation. Original private replacement/typing/source guards and no-send boundaries remain in place.
4. **Saved reply continuation from Home and Talk.** Exactly six Home areas remain. A single eligible original saved reply can be reopened with its exact customer/enquiry; multiple candidates are not guessed. Current unfinished workspace text takes priority. Original typed/already-transcribed routing supports continuing the reply the owner was writing, including saved replies. Current role, source, revision, record and typing checks reject stale actions. Navigation is not saving, sending or task completion.
5. **Source-removal and interrupted-save recovery.** Original customer details expose an authorised saved private reply even if its original enquiry is removed or changed. Wording remains editable with an explicit source-changed warning; source-dependent questions, save and return actions are disabled until proper current-source preparation. No new enquiry/customer is created. Foreign owner/business/role records are hidden. Permission-interrupted saves become uncertain instead of remaining stuck saving; restored authority can check the original bytes without repeating the write.
