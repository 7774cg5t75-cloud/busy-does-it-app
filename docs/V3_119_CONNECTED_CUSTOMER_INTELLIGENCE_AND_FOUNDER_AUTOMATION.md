# V3.119 — Connected website customer journey and founder priorities

## Customer simplicity and end-to-end proof
A shared read-only website journey assistant turns existing strict business details, private draft, hosted preview, approval and matching public health evidence into simple next instructions and a six-step progress display. Running website jobs, outdated previews and unverified public deployments have different actions. The assistant never publishes, registers domains or certifies the customer's live site from a preview or database row. The old strict public-domain proof remains the only route to an "online and verified" claim.

## Per-business Business Brain coaching
A private learning explanation now shows why BUSY recommended a style. Explicit liked/kept choices may influence the next suggested family for that business, but never become claims about model weight training, conversion lift or platform-wide learning. Missing verified preferences fall back to industry-context wording; priority business facts take precedence over design polish. The existing clear-preferences option remains available, and no personal data is added to a shared dataset.

## Design critique remains evidence-based
The existing before/after measured review classifier is reused: improvement in browser-measured usability is different from a merely safe alternative. New browser tests compare truly rendered fictional industry websites at phone/desktop widths, verify that business wording remains intact, test withheld public-delivery evidence and retain accessibility negative controls. No paid multimodal reviewer or automatic customer-site patching is switched on.

## Founder action with minimal owner workload
Founder Operations now gives a single safe next check from server-authorised aggregate incidents and freshness evidence, rather than implying no incident simply because a monitor is stale. It is a read-only recommendation, not a live phone notification, provider action, subscription upgrade, repair or publishing replay.

## Guardrails and scope
The seven-gate isolated fictional sandbox checklist and eleven-gate Global-Ready pre-release checklist stay deny-by-default. No actual canary was launched. V3.115/V3.117 private learning migrations remain undeployed. No customer DNS, real domain purchase, paid AI calls, signed iPhone build or public release has occurred.

GitHub CI runs integrated contract checks, iPhone JavaScript bundling, actual Chromium customer-site screenshots, an independent before/after quality comparison, five-industry design diversity, responsive/mobile and accessible keyboard checks. This demonstrates development-source integrity, not customer production readiness.
