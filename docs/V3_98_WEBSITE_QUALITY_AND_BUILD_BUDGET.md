# V3.98 — Better customer website quality (credit-conscious sweep)

## Customer experience
- A compact optional **Make your website stronger** coaching card highlights one high-impact missing business fact. A **See more website tips** control reveals further suggestions on demand. Advice is derived from confirmed existing business information — never fabricated reviews, contact details, prices or photos.
- The signed private hosted-site viewer offers scrollable **Home / Services / About / ...** page controls. Each page is fetched through the original exact-deployment signed manifest; external websites and unknown page links remain blocked.
- Preview viewer header uses the customer business name recorded with the preview, rather than generic BUSY marketing copy.
- The customer's optional **logo label** travels from Brand & Business Identity into the generated website as an escaped text wordmark. When absent, the customer business name is used. This is **not** a logo-file upload or image creation feature. The BUSY product brand remains in the app's own navigation, not as an automatic brand on the customer's website.
- Existing preview-again navigation (V3.97), separate public approval and V3.95 script-disabled HTML viewer safeguards are retained.
