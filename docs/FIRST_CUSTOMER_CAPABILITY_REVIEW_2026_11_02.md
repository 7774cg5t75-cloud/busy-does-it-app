Shared Busy intelligence already supplies scoped business profile, Brand DNA and existing content/owner corrections. Finite goal recognisers route selected outcomes; they are not unrestricted understanding and cannot grant their own execution authority. Provider-neutral proposals and the central budget controller are foundations for future authorised model calls, not live capability in this mission. General bespoke generated-code execution, universal autonomous workflows, fully activated providers and public-ready individual standalone apps remain ambitions/incomplete authority paths rather than features proven by these tests.

Recommended next missions, in order:

1. **First website that earns customer confidence.** Secure real browser/mobile rendering and licensed/owner-approved image evidence; compare actual before/after output with approved visual references and professional judgement. Improve guided business facts/photo choice and visual recovery, keeping free and paid quality identical. Hosted acquisition activation, domains and spending need separate approval and acceptance. This is the nearest first-use value with the lowest new authority burden.
2. **One connected natural-goal journey.** Reuse approved business facts across Website, App, Content and Growth; keep owner wording, expose only essential choices, and carry a reviewed draft to an independently verified supported outcome. Finite unsupported responses remain honest. Any paid model integration must use existing trusted proposal/admission controls and separate authorisation. This reduces friction across all six areas without duplicating stores/controllers.
3. **Genuine Work after the enquiry.** Design/implement the narrow original canonical job authority with exact source/quote/acceptance evidence, current permissions, CAS, idempotent receipts, unknown-outcome recovery and explicit owner/customer boundaries. Prove archived generations before long-running operational use. Do not infer jobs from checklists, issue invoices or move money through this transition. This unlocks the daily operational value behind bespoke owner apps once the authority is genuinely ready.

Release remains separately gated by hosted JWT/RLS/storage and rollback, public privacy/consent/retention/erasure, provider-specific acceptance, actual native/browser UX/accessibility and appropriate independent assurance.
