# Original Work progression and operation lifetime — review proposal, 2 November2026

The implemented current-state summary distinguishes original filed enquiries, original Prepared quotes and private owner preparations. Customer acceptance, an authorised job, delivery/completion and financial outcomes are not established by checklist progress. The original quote, customer and enquiry IDs remain distinct. Later quote preparation references the existing enquiry progress; the original duplicate-progress denial remains authoritative. No transition below is enabled by this proposal.

A future genuine job must be an explicit finite extension of canonical Work, with its own immutable identity, exact business/customer/enquiry/quote associations and approved source revisions. Proposed states need separate semantics: proposed operational job; authorised operational job; in progress; completion awaiting verification; independently verified completion; cancellation with retained history. Customer acceptance, where required, must have its own verifiable original evidence and applicability (exact quote terms/revision, identity, expiry, revocation), never inferred from a note, owner checkbox or matching contact. Owner approval is independently required for the exact transition. No accepted state is inferred for quote-free work either.

Every transition needs current transactional membership/source checks, version/CAS, exact one-use request identity, bounded immutable transition receipts, independently readable results, unknown-outcome reconciliation without replay, revocation/retaining rollback and privacy/retention. Preparing or completing a job must not itself send, publish, invoice or charge. Those actions must call their existing separately authorised authorities with separate approval, idempotency, spending and evidence. Staff permissions and external notifications require distinct contracts; they do not derive from an operational App screen.

## Existing operation cap: retained

A plan can have at most512 accepted save/Undo/source-review operations, with five Undo snapshots. Read/overview and App placement operations do not consume these nonces. A small enquiry has considerable capacity, but a long-running daily-updated plan can reach the cap (for example, two accepted edits per day reach512 in256 days). Further writes, including Undo and source-review, then fail closed; the current plan remains readable and no nonce is recycled. Saving another App placement cannot reset it. This mission tests exhaustion/retained reads, not an unlimited-edit claim.

A later archived-generation design should introduce an immutable generation ID, immutable terminal snapshot/hash and retained nonce/receipt index for every closed generation; one source-scoped CAS head points to exactly one active successor. Deliberate owner review must bind the final old revision, exact retained plan and new generation before a finite transactional archive operation. The archive receipt must include both generation IDs and head revisions; unknown archive outcomes require exact readback, never retry. All subsequent operations must name their generation so an old request cannot execute in the successor. Foreign/current membership, stale archive, concurrent archive/edit, nonce reuse across generations, retained evidence and rollback tests are mandatory. No histories or nonces are deleted to manufacture capacity. This proposal requires separately reviewed original-authority SQL/client/server versioning and is not implemented or installed.

Private App placements currently keep32 immutable placement versions per App, consistent with the existing finite private-screen pattern. This is a separate cap, not the Work nonce ledger. At the cap, changed placement/removal must also refuse further writes; reads/history remain. Production acceptance must review suitable archival and retention before long-running customer use. No hosted data or schema change is authorised by this document.
