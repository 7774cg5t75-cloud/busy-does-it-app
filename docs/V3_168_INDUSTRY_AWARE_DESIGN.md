# V3.168 — Intelligent Industry App Creation 6.0 (development)

**Branch:** `v3.168` (branched from `v3.167`)  
**Scope:** Private customer-facing Business App preview, not a published app.

## Delivered in source control

1. **Owner-selected business type.** The customer preview now offers *Let Busy review*, *Appointments*, *Trades & services*, *Food & events* and *Retail*. Selection is local preview state, never inferred from private customer data, and resets when switching to another app. By default, the existing V3.167 suggestions remain unchanged.
2. **Distinct industry design hypotheses.** The pure `src/core/appIndustryOrchestrator.mjs` module generates finite, supported appearance alternatives for different needs. These hints have lower priority than rendered measurements and are filtered through existing V3.167 owner-feedback suppression and approved-style protection. Unsupported modules or publication actions cannot be generated by the planner.
3. **Combined owner-aware preview.** The existing native Customer Preview accepts owner-selected industry alternatives into the original-versus-proposed review flow. Existing reversible coordinated editing, virtual-width capture, before/after measurement and owner approval boundaries are preserved. An owner's rejection reloads scoped feedback before another suggestion is prepared; if private history is unavailable, the industry planner does not produce another potentially repeated recommendation.
4. **Explicit customer-journey readiness.** The owner can see whether booking requests, enquiries/contact and services are configured. The UI states that a booking request is **not** a confirmed diary booking; enquiry delivery and replies still require end-to-end checks. The module does not enable any feature, submit requests, message customers or change payments.
5. **Three-width design preflight.** The baseline and proposed private designs are compared using the existing deterministic responsive rules at 320, 390 and 820 logical layout points. Added/removed rule warnings are reported alongside their limitations. This is *not* screenshot, physical-device or accessibility verification.
6. **Regression/packaging workflow.** New `scripts/check-app-industry-v3168.mjs` covers distinct outputs by industry, safe Undo, tenant-data exclusion, owner preference priority, business workflow labels, bounded inputs, and UI/workflow integration. New `.github/workflows/app-industry-orchestrator-v3168.yml` runs new and historical tests, native-source syntax checks, Busy iOS/Android exports and independent standalone iOS/Android exports.

## Approval and privacy boundaries

- No draft is saved until the owner explicitly approves it through the existing private workflow.
- No app is automatically published or submitted to either app store.
- No booking is confirmed; no enquiry is sent; no customer record or payment is modified.
- Suggestions use finite `field/value` style options; industry selection is not inferred from personal details.
- Owner feedback remains scoped by the existing authenticated business-specific service. This feature does not retrain model weights or combine businesses' records.
- Industry preferences are *hypotheses*, not validated conversion optimisations.
- The existing V3.167 visual comparison includes an opt-in AI screenshot path. V3.168's deterministic preflight does not inspect screenshot pixels.
