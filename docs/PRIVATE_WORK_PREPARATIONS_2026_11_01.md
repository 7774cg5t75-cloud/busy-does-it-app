## What owners can now do

An owner can describe catering, gardening or creative-project preparation, inspect a finite suggested plan, edit its title/stage/task wording, add/remove stages and tasks, reorder stages, check/uncheck tasks, and explicitly change preparation-stage status. The owner can ask bounded changes such as moving equipment earlier, removing a materials stage, marking an exact named task, adding a task to a named stage, or showing what is left. Ambiguous and unsupported instructions are refused; this is finite recognition, not general AI interpretation.

The reusable contract supports arbitrary owner-entered stage/task wording; these are three starting suggestions, not separate industry execution handlers. All plans use the same original Work surface, interpreter-free finite validation and persistence authority. Eight stages/twelve tasks each, globally unique finite identities, bounded text and payloads, boolean completion and explicit todo/current/done preparation states are enforced. Only one stage may be current; done stages require checked tasks; direct todo↔done jumps are refused. Empty/unknown/executable fields and malformed structures fail validation.

Changes remain private proposals until an exact owner review. The review displays the proposed plan, including task completion. Cancelling invalidates the ticket and cached approval callback. Existing original Keep/Discard navigation protects unfinished wording; attempting to replace a dirty plan without that authority refuses rather than overwriting it. Account, business, customer, engagement, quote-version and component-lifetime guards reject stale callbacks. Original customer wording is displayed separately from owner planning notes.

After a deliberate save, success needs an exact original operation receipt plus independent authoritative readback of the whole plan. Closing and reopening restores checked progress. Undo is a separate review of the actual previous server plan and compares its independent readback. No preparation completion changes quote/customer lifecycle or executes an external action.
