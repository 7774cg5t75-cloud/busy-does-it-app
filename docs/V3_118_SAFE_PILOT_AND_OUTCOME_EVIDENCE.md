# V3.118 — controlled fictional pilot preparation and honest outcomes

## Closed-pilot readiness, not a deployed test
Added a seven-gate, deny-by-default test readiness helper. It requires an isolated local/sandbox environment without production credentials; exact matching business UUID and owner/admin authority; fictional business details and approved test media; independently checked tenant isolation; dry run with no external writes or paid calls; reviewed rollback and test-data deletion; and explicit founder approval. Even with all seven true, the result is only "ready for manual sandbox rehearsal" and cannot provision a sandbox, publish a real website, send messages, process a payment, or contact a customer.

The Founder Operations screen shows pilot test progress as unverified (0 of 7) until independent evidence is provided through a future authorised workflow. The 11-point master Global-Ready pre-release audit remains separately blocked.

## Outcome evidence remains factual and private
The per-business feedback history now returns the latest explicitly reported keep/revert decision with the exact draft revision. Customer-facing Website Builder guidance applies it to the current design only when both style and revision match. Otherwise it merely states that earlier preferences were saved. No visitor traffic or sales claims.

An additional evidence evaluator accepts authenticated matching tenant IDs, a genuinely measured before/after website audit, and matching owner-reported feedback. It keeps browser findings and owner opinions separate. It does not publish, auto-apply, calculate conversion lift or claim an increase in income. Tests use fictional business websites rendered in Chromium, compare actual layouts and deliberately worsen touch targets to ensure rejection.

The existing owner-feedback cloud migration is still undeployed, and no cross-business learning or model retraining occurs.

## Founder intervention reduction
The Founder Operations priority list now checks how old the aggregate platform snapshot is. With trustworthy supplied time evidence, a report older than 24 hours becomes "needs refreshing" even if its recorded counts are zero. Missing or future timestamps are unverified. Recorded incidents remain actionable but no privileged action is taken: no social retries, domain changes, automatic billing, customer data exposure or external notifications.

## QA and boundary
The development branch runs the older website, privacy and security regressions, iOS JavaScript compilation, actual Chromium before/after design review, screenshot identity and covered-control checks, five independent fictional styles, responsive widths and keyboard accessibility. Screenshots and audit evidence are retained as temporary GitHub workflow artifacts.

No real customer pilot, live sandbox provision, production Supabase migration, registrar integration, paid AI model, signed native iOS build or public release was performed. A future production rollout must separately approve a canary, run the migrations with rollback, inspect data retention and deletion, and audit actual operational readiness.
