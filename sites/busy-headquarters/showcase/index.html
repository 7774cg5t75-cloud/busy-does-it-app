Good questions, clear answers

Everything you might
want to know.

How much does exterior cleaning cost?

Costs vary with surface material, area, access and the level of cleaning needed. A real service provider should assess the job and confirm a clear quote before work begins.

Do I need to be at home?

That depends on the property, water access and security arrangements. A real business would agree access details before any visit.

Can you clean delicate stone or render?

Not every surface is suitable for high-pressure cleaning. A qualified service provider should check the material and choose an appropriate method.

Can I book or request a real quote on this demo?

No. ClearFlow is a fictional business. This site is a design showcase, not a trading business; there are no real bookings, quotes or contact submissions.
