THE FOUNDER STORY

Built by a small-business owner,
for small-business owners.

Busy didn't start as an idea in a boardroom. It grew from the challenges of running a real small business.

Between long days trading with Slow Roast at festivals, customers to look after and endless everyday jobs, the idea was simple: what if you could have the support of a team without having to employ one?

That's what we're working to build: practical AI technology that gives independent business owners more freedom, not another thing to manage.

Made with ambition in Devon, UK.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious about Busy?

Can I subscribe to Busy Does It yet?

Not yet. Busy is in active development. We'll confirm pricing, availability and supported features when they're ready; this site doesn't collect payments or sign-ups.

Is Busy only for one type of business?

No. It's being designed for sole traders and small teams across trades, hospitality, retail, creative services and more.

Is the sample website a real customer site?

No. It's a clearly fictional demonstration of an existing Busy website-draft capability — not proof of a customer deployment or live AI generation on this webpage.

Will Busy do things without my approval?

Important customer-facing actions, spending and publication are designed to require clear permissions and review. We'll be transparent about what is actually implemented.
