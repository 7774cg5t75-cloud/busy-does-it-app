A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious about Busy?

Find quick answers to the things you might be wondering.

01What exactly is Busy Does It?

Busy Does It is an AI business assistant we're building for small-business owners. It's designed to help create websites and apps, attract customers, organise everyday work and get paid — like having a helpful team behind your business, without employing one.

02Do I need to understand AI or be good with technology?

Not at all. Just tell Busy what you need, by speaking or typing. It's being designed for people who run businesses, not people who work in technology.

03Can Busy build a professional website for my business?

That's one of Busy's main features. Our goal is to let you describe your business, see a professional website, request changes and approve the finished result — without needing to write code. The Website Builder is currently in development.

04Can Busy create an app specifically for my business?

That's what we're working towards. You could describe the kind of app you want — for bookings, customer services or an idea of your own — and review it as Busy puts it together. The App Builder is still in development.

05Can Busy help me find more customers and grow my business?

That's one of the main things we're building. Busy aims to help with advertising, social posts, being found online and spotting opportunities for new work. You'll stay in control of what gets published and what you spend.

06Can Busy help manage customers, bookings, quotes and invoices?

That's the plan. Busy is being designed to bring enquiries, bookings, quotes and invoices together, so you spend less time organising and more time serving customers.

07Is Busy suitable for my type of business?

Busy is being built for people working on their own or in small teams — from trades and gardening to beauty, food, retail and creative businesses. The aim is to fit how you work, rather than make you fit a template.

08Will Busy make decisions or spend money without my permission?

You're in control. Busy is being designed to ask for your approval before important actions such as sending messages, publishing changes or spending money, and to show you what has actually happened.

09How much will Busy cost, and when can I try it?

We're developing affordable subscription plans for small businesses, alongside a way to preview what Busy could create before paying. Final prices, included features and launch availability haven't been confirmed yet. We'll explain everything clearly before subscriptions open.
