GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Is BUSY DOES IT available to subscribe to?

Not yet. The platform is in active development, and we are completing reliability, privacy, payment and App Store readiness checks before opening sign-ups.

Is this only for one type of trade?

No. BUSY is being built for different types of small business, including service trades and other independent businesses.

Will BUSY publish or message customers without my approval?

The aim is to keep important public actions under your control, with clear permission and review steps.

Can I use my own domain?

Support for customer-owned domains is part of the website platform design. Domain verification and actual live publishing are subject to setup and testing.
