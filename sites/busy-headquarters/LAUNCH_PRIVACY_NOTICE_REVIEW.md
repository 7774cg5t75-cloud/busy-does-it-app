## What information we collect
The coming-soon website is available without an account or payment. Automatic contact submissions, subscriptions and automated waiting-list registration remain OFF.

If someone voluntarily sends an email, we receive the sender's email address, supplied name, message and any information voluntarily included. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive customer information.

Cloudflare provides the website and may process access/security log information such as IP address, browser and request details. The fictional ClearFlow demonstration uses images delivered from Unsplash; viewing the images may cause requests to that provider.

## Why we use it
We use voluntarily sent correspondence to reply to enquiries and consider feedback. The proposed lawful basis is legitimate interests in handling ordinary enquiries, or steps requested before a potential business relationship where applicable. This must be approved for the actual operation before publication.

An email expressing interest in early access is NOT an automatic signup or permission for a general marketing newsletter. A future opt-in system will require a separate privacy and consent review before activation.
