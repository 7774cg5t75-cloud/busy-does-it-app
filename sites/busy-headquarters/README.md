# V3.80 — BUSY DOES IT Headquarters and First Website Demonstration

**Status: prepared for review only. NOT published to busydoesit.co.uk, demo.busydoesit.co.uk, or any public host.**

The `sites/busy-headquarters/` folder contains the website files:
- `index.html`: official BUSY DOES IT marketing homepage; responsive, semantic, and honest about development status.
- `styles.css`: responsive visual design and keyboard/reduced-motion accessibility support.
- `privacy.html`: clearly labelled **unapproved placeholder**, intentionally `noindex`. Must be replaced with a legally reviewed and accurate privacy notice before public launch.
- `demo-brand-brain.mjs`: fictional home-and-garden business brief using BUSY's real `buildWebsiteDraft` input structure.
- `demo/index.html`: website **generated from that brief by the existing BUSY website-draft renderer**, not an invented rendering or a deployed customer site.

## Architecture & ownership
- The main `busydoesit.co.uk` is the platform marketing/entry website, not a customer's website. It must support public explanations, pricing, future account registration, subscriptions and legal terms. The marketing HTML is separately authored while these SaaS-specific capabilities are being designed. **Do not claim our main marketing website was built automatically by the customer website builder.**
- The customer website system provides an immutable hosted preview, explicit owner Go Live, managed subdomain, Cloudflare routing/SSL and health verification when real infrastructure and permission are in place.
- The **fictional demo website** was generated by BUSY's website builder; it has no contact details, customer ID, real bookings, artificial testimonials, public enquiry form or deployment. The synthetic business brief is isolated from real user cloud workspace data.
- `demo.busydoesit.co.uk` is an intended **future approved staging host**, not a verified reservation or live address. The default route should be managed through the same website worker/cloud hosting mechanisms customers use.
- No DNS, domain nameserver, Cloudflare route, live Supabase site, account or payment settings were changed by this sweep.

## Official marketing site safety
- `index.html` and `privacy.html` include `robots=noindex,nofollow` while in development.
- The draft does not have a live signup, login or subscription checkout, and does not falsely suggest payment collection.
- Prices, feature availability and usage credits remain provisional; no hard-coded billing tiers.
- Features in development are clearly labelled; no fabricated testimonials, growth numbers or public success claims.
- No visitor analytics JavaScript, third-party media, lead-collection forms or service keys were inserted.
