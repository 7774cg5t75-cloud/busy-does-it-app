PRIVACY & TRUST

Privacy notice — draft

This is an unfinished legal draft, not an approved privacy notice. Automatic website forms remain disabled until server security settings and this privacy information are approved. When enabled, a contact form will send an enquiry through a trusted email provider. For early-access interest, the website will store a temporary pending email address to send a verification link, and it will only mark the address as subscribed after that link is confirmed. This draft must be reviewed and replaced before public collection begins.

Contact emails and expressions of interest

The contact form prepares a message in your own email app; it is not submitted through this website. If you choose to send an email to hello@busydoesit.co.uk, the address, message and details you include will be received through our email provider and used to respond to your enquiry. An email expressing interest in early access is not an automatic registration, account or guaranteed invitation. Do not include sensitive information. Contact us at that address if you want your enquiry deleted. Any later mailing list requires a reviewed policy, lawful consent process and secure backend before activation.

Future early-access registration

When the secure form is activated, an applicant who requests an early-access notification must check an affirmative consent box and confirm ownership of the email address. A pending record is created before confirmation solely so a verification link can be sent; it is not eligible for marketing. We will keep the confirmed address to send one launch notification, not a general newsletter. We will not sell the list. Unconfirmed registration attempts must be deleted after seven days; confirmed records must be retained only as necessary until launch or until deletion is requested. Contact hello@busydoesit.co.uk for access or deletion. A final approved privacy notice and privacy operations are required before activation.

About this preview

This static draft uses no analytics scripts or marketing cookies. Future hosting may produce normal server access logs, which must be explained in an approved policy before launch.

Before this website goes public

We need to confirm the legal operating entity and controller contact, data purposes and legal bases, processors, any transfers, security measures, retention periods, data rights processes, cookie choices and complaints routes.

Busy Does It customer data

The full platform is in development. Any customer records, AI features, payments or third-party integrations will have distinct privacy and operational requirements before activation.
