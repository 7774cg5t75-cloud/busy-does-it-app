Contact emails and expressions of interest

The website displays a contact email link. If you choose to send a message, your email app handles sending it; the website itself does not collect a form submission. If you choose to send an email to hello@busydoesit.co.uk, the address, message and details you include will be received through our email provider and used to respond to your enquiry. An email expressing interest in early access is not an automatic registration, account or guaranteed invitation. Do not include sensitive information. Contact us at that address if you want your enquiry deleted. Any later mailing list requires a reviewed policy, lawful consent process and secure backend before activation.

Potential future early-access registration (not active)

When the secure form is activated, an applicant who requests an early-access notification must check an affirmative consent box and confirm ownership of the email address. A pending record is created before confirmation solely so a verification link can be sent; it is not eligible for marketing. We will keep the confirmed address to send one launch notification, not a general newsletter. We will not sell the list. Unconfirmed registration attempts must be deleted after seven days; confirmed records must be retained only as necessary until launch or until deletion is requested. Contact hello@busydoesit.co.uk for access or deletion. A final approved privacy notice and privacy operations are required before activation.
